Privacy Policy – Wanted Level Clipper

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Wanted Level Clipper is a personal tool run on its owner's own computer. It is used only by the owner of the Wanted Level YouTube channel.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to read basic channel information and upload videos to the owner's own channel.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Google's.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of YouTube data follows the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
